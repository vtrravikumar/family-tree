# Vansvriksh — Friends & Family UAT Guide

**Status:** Draft for the controlled Friends & Family beta  
**Audience:** Invited testers  
**Purpose:** Help a small group of real families try Vansvriksh and report useful findings.

## Welcome

Thank you for helping test Vansvriksh. This is an early, limited beta. The application is usable, but some workflows may be rough and defects are expected. Your feedback will help us improve it before a wider release.

This is a private test, not a public launch. Please use only the family workspace(s) you have been invited to access.

## Before you start

- Sign in with the Google account whose email address received the invitation.
- Open the invitation link and follow the acceptance flow. Invitations expire after seven days; ask the family Owner/Admin for a fresh invitation if yours has expired.
- Check the role assigned to you: Owner, Admin, Editor or Viewer. Your role controls which actions are available.
- If you cannot sign in or the invitation does not work, contact the person who invited you.

## What to try

Please try the tasks relevant to your role:

1. Sign in and open the family workspace.
2. Browse the family member directory and family tree.
3. Open a person profile and review the information shown.
4. If your role permits it, add or edit a person and create or update a relationship.
5. Check whether the actions available to you match your assigned role.
6. If you are an Owner or Admin, try inviting a trusted tester with an appropriate role and confirm the invitation flow.
7. Sign out and sign in again.

Do not deliberately test access to another family's records using real personal data. If you notice another family's information where it should not appear, stop using the application and report it immediately.

## Data and privacy precautions

Family information is personal. During this beta:

- Enter only information you are comfortable sharing with the people who have access to that family workspace.
- Do not enter government identifiers, financial details, passwords, medical records or other highly sensitive information.
- Use care with dates, biographies, photographs and details about living people. Obtain their permission where appropriate before adding personal details.
- Do not share invitation links outside the intended test group.
- Do not post screenshots containing names, email addresses, dates of birth, family relationships or other personal information in public channels.
- Report a suspected privacy or access-control issue promptly and include only the minimum information needed to explain it.

## What to report

Please report both problems and confusing experiences. Useful feedback includes:

- What you were trying to do.
- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened.
- The page or feature where it happened.
- Whether it happened once or every time.
- Your assigned role (Owner/Admin/Editor/Viewer).
- Browser and device type, if relevant.

Avoid sending passwords, session cookies, invitation URLs, private family spreadsheets or full personal records in a report. If a screenshot helps, redact personal details first.

## Known beta limitations and expectations

- This is a limited beta. Availability, interface details and workflows may change as issues are fixed.
- A Google sign-in session expires after seven days unless you sign out earlier.
- Family invitations expire after seven days.
- Signing out ends the current session on that browser.
- The beta does not promise a particular support response time or service availability.
- Please keep your own copy of important family information. Do not treat the beta as the sole archive or backup of irreplaceable records.

## Support and urgent reports

For normal feedback or help, contact the person who invited you to the beta.

For a suspected cross-family data exposure, unauthorized access, missing/changed family data or other serious security concern:
1. Stop using the affected workflow.
2. Do not try to investigate by accessing other records or repeating destructive actions.
3. Contact the person who invited you as soon as possible, describing what happened without forwarding sensitive records.
4. Preserve the approximate time and the steps that led to the issue.

## Thank you

Your time and honest feedback are valuable. Please focus on using Vansvriksh naturally with your family rather than trying to perform a formal security audit. We will review reported issues, fix confirmed defects and share important updates with the beta group.
